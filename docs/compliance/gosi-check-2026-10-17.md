### Two findings that change the design
1. **The cohort comes from the person's GOSI history, not from their hire date.**
   - The new law does **not** apply to "Contributors who have contribution periods prior to the effective date of the Law for which they have not received compensations" (Royal Decree, Clause Second(1)).
   - For that group, the old Social Insurance Law M/33, the Civil Pension Law and the UIL "shall continue to apply" (Clause Fourth).
   - So a Saudi hired by us in 2026 who has an old contribution record that was never paid out is on the old law.
2. **The annuities step-up runs on calendar dates from the law's effective date. It does not run per new entrant.**
   - The text: "18 percent for the 12 months following the effective date of the Law … 19 percent as of the month following the lapse of 12 months …", up to 22% after 48 months. Each step is split 50/50 between employer and employee (Clause Third(1), (2)).
   - In force: "as of the day following the date of its issuance" (Art. 63), which is 2024-07-03.
   - So every new-law contributor pays the same rate in a given month. That rate is **20% (10% + 10%) for August 2026 to June 2027** under either reading of "month following the lapse" (see Unclear 1).
   - The spec's March 2027 examples should therefore use 20% for a new-law Saudi.
