# Opening-balance dry run, 2026-10-21 (fictional data)

Prepared by the migration-agent. **Every amount, name and number here is fictional**: the dry-run fixture
`migration/fixtures/dry_run.py`. No Almiras or Falcon data was read, and no real export is committed. This report
shows that the migration path works. It is not the tie-out of a real load, so the accountant does not sign it off.

Run: `migration/test_dry_run.py` (7 tests) on SQLite, through the same commands and services the runbook (spec §10)
uses. The files are written to a scratch folder, not to git. *Updated 2026-10-21 (second pass, gaps 1 to 9 closed or
narrowed, see the end):* the run now loads the sub-ledgers with the real loaders (`migration_subledgers`), approves
the map and posts the opening entries through the approval engine, and both AR/AP-bearing opening entries post only

## What the dry run proved

1. **Workbook (W4 to W17).** Both TBs balance to the halala. Every code with a balance is mapped to a postable ERP
   account of the right class. Every sub-ledger tie-out above is zero, the bai' ajel contracts included. The
   purification account equals the charity payable, the intercompany balances mirror each other across the two
   entities, and the workbook's Sharia flags equal the ERP entities'. The only warning is `tax_balance_evidence`
   on 2200 (agree the VAT balance to the last return, W15).
2. **Falcon journal to TB (R11).** For each of the 20 codes, the TB opening plus the journal's December movement equals
   the TB closing (journal total 209,932.11). The CSV TB closing equals the workbook TB code by code.
3. **Mapping (R12, R13).** The map drafted from the workbook equals the CSV map. The preparer submits it, which opens
   its request in the approval engine; the preparer cannot approve it; a second person approves it through the
   engine.
4. **Loading (R14).** A dry run writes nothing. The applied run drafts one entry per entity and submits it for
   approval (a `ledger.manual_entry` request). Each draft is balanced, every line has one side only with 2 decimals,
   and every account belongs to the entity. The 1210 line carries the counterparty DRYS. A re-run skips the entry and
   creates no duplicate.
5. **Sub-ledgers (R23).** Customers, vendors and 11 open items load into invoicing as migrated open items (pre-go-live,
   never sent to ZATCA, no journal entry; the USD item keeps USD 2,000.00 and SAR 7,500.00); 3 SAR bank accounts load
   as drafts; 2 employees, their end-of-service openings and a leave opening load into hr and payroll; the 2 bai'
   ajel contracts load sold, with 14 and 9 instalments still to collect from January 2027. Each section is loaded by
   the person holding its right; a re-run loads nothing twice.
6. **The posting gate (B2, M6, M7, R8, R22).** The opening entry may carry 1200, 1210, 2100, 2110, 2500, 1240 and
   1241 balances. It posts only after they tie out to what is loaded:
   - With nothing loaded, posting is refused.
   - A load 0.01 short on 1200 is refused (en/ar, with account and amount), also when the draft is decided straight
     from the approval engine's inbox; nothing posts.
   - The exact load posts.
   - Replaying the Falcon journal is refused, because 4 of its entries touch 1201 or 2101.
7. **Posting (R16).** The preparer cannot post. The approver posts both entries through the approval engine (the
   request approved under the code default, decision logged), and each goes through `ledger.services.post_entry`,
   which a spy confirmed. After posting the gated sub-ledgers are not loaded again.
8. **Restricted accounts.** 1140 (purification) and the bai' ajel accounts 1240, 1241 and 4610 accept the opening
   entry, which uses the migration allowance. After posting, a manual journal on any of the four is refused
   (`restricted_account`).
9. **Sharia per entity (rule 5, OQ-10).** DRYS does not enforce Sharia, and its 2410 loan and 5610 interest load. The
   same 2410 balance in DRYH is an error in the workbook check and in the loader, and nothing is created.
10. **Entity separation (rule 4, R19, R24).** A DRYS-only accountant cannot map or load DRYH, and cannot run the
    workbook check over both entities. A DRYH map row cannot point to a DRYS account. The intercompany lines mirror
    each other: DRYH 1210 (DRYS) + DRYS 2110 (DRYH) = 0, in the workbook (W17) and in the posted ledger.
11. **Rounding.** A SAR amount with 3 decimals is refused, in the workbook and in the TB CSV. It is never rounded. A
    USD open item with 3 decimals is refused (`currency_decimals`).

## Code changes made for this dry run (spec amended 2026-10-21)

- **R8 as amended (AT-23).** On the opening entry, control accounts are listed as an info, not a warning. In a
  replayed journal, each entry that touches a control account is an error naming the entry and the account, and the
  run is refused.
- **R16 as amended.** A replayed import draft with a control-account line is refused at posting.
- **R22 (AT-24).** The posting gate `_open_item_tie_out` compares the entry with the open items reported by sources
  registered through `register_open_items_source`. Until a source is registered, an entry with control-account
  balances is refused.
- **W14 (new).** A 1240 or 1241 balance gives a warning, because no contract detail is held. A 1241 debit balance, or
  a 1241 credit larger than 1240, is an error.
- **Existing tests.** The shared fixture journal no longer replays lines on 1201. The tests that post an opening
  entry now load matching open items.

## Gaps for the real Falcon load (need a builder or a human decision)

Status after the second pass (2026-10-21): **closed** = built and tested; **narrowed** = built in part, the rest
listed.

1. **R23 open-item loader.** *Closed.* Open AR/AP load into invoicing as migrated open items (`MigratedOpenItem`, the
   migration document type, pre-go-live, no ZATCA, no entry) and are the R22 source. Remaining: a receipt or payment
   after go-live cannot yet settle a migrated item (invoicing OQ-79).
2. **Approval engine (R13, R16, AT-25).** *Closed.* The map is submitted and decided in the engine; a direct
   `approve_account_map` call is refused (`approval_request_required`). The loaded drafts are submitted
   (`ledger.manual_entry`) and posted by the engine's decision; the import checks also run inside the ledger posting,
   so the inbox cannot bypass them.
3. **Falcon TB and chart export formats (OQ-1).** *Open.* The TB CSV column map is a guess. The real export has to be
   profiled under `exports/` by an authorised person; an agent never sees it.
4. **Bai' ajel contracts.** *Closed* (CFO decision taken on the team's recommendation). The workbook has a Bai' ajel
   contracts sheet; 1240 and 1241 tie to it (W14, an error without contract rows); the loader creates the contracts
   sold in payroll with their remaining instalments and registers the sales in invoicing. Remaining: an ibra' rebate
   on a migrated contract is refused (invoicing OQ-80).
5. **Other module loaders (OQ-12).** *Narrowed.* SAR bank accounts (drafts), employees (drafts), EOSB openings and
   leave openings load; EOSB openings gate the 2500 line. Remaining: the fixed-asset register (there is no
   fixed-assets module), employee contracts (their terms are not in the workbook), foreign-currency bank accounts
   (gap 6).
6. **Foreign currency (OQ-13).** *Narrowed.* Migrated open items keep the document-currency amount and the SAR amount;
   the opening entry and the tie-outs use SAR (the recommended default). Remaining: revaluation of those items, and
   foreign-currency bank accounts, whose currency GL sub-account cannot take a SAR opening line (an FX opening line is
   to build).
7. **Control-account lists.** *Closed.* One list (`ledger.control_accounts`, from the chart template): 1200, 1210, 2100,
   2110; 2310 and 2320 dropped. VAT 1310 / 2200 and WHT 2220 are tax control accounts, evidenced against the last
   returns (W15, a warning per balance). The ledger refusal of manual journals on control accounts (invoicing R97)
   is still to build.
8. **Purification.** *Closed* for the opening: 1140 above 2600 is an error, below it a warning to fund (W16). No
   purification register is migrated.
9. **Two cross-checks.** *Closed.* The workbook check compares intercompany mirrors across entities, and the ERP
   entity's Sharia flag governs the workbook's (W17).
10. **Year replay (OQ-4, OQ-5, OQ-14).** *Open, default in force.* Closing TB plus the R23 sub-ledgers is the path built;
    movements after the cut-over are entered in the ERP (no replay of `مستخلص عميل`).
11. **Sharia-prohibited legacy balances in a Sharia entity (OQ-10)** block the load. The Sharia board or the CFO
    decides how they are treated.
12. **PostgreSQL.** *Open.* The run was on SQLite only. The PostgreSQL guards (e.g. `invoicing/0023` restricted
    accounts, the new `invoicing/0034` migrated-item trigger) were not exercised.
