# Labour Law and WPS check of the [CC-verify] items in the HR and payroll specs

**Bottom line:** 9 items in the specs contradict the in-force text and need a spec change (listed in section 3). The biggest ones:
- **Probation (hr R17):** the law has a single 180-day maximum. There is no 90-day limit with a written extension to 180.
- **Non-Saudi contracts (hr R16):** an open-ended contract for a non-Saudi must be refused, not just warned. Regs Art. 12 says it never becomes open-ended, even if both parties agree.
- **Resignation at exactly 5 years (hr R37):** the award is 1/3, not 2/3.
- **Maternity pay:** 12 weeks at full pay. Pay no longer depends on length of service.
- **Calendar (hr R39):** the law counts every period in the Hijri calendar unless the contract says otherwise (Art. 10). The unified Qiwa contract picks Gregorian (cl. 14.6).
- **Overtime cap:** there is a statutory cap of 720 hours a year (Regs Art. 22). Payroll OQ-5 assumes no cap.

GOSI could not be checked at all (section 4).
