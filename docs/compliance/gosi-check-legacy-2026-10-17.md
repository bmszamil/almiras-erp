## GOSI check, second pass: the legacy cohort (`legacy_m33`) and the remaining Unclear items (2026-10-17)

The cms/ booklets settle most of the legacy-cohort figures. GOSI's own 2025 booklet of the old law M/33 confirms 9%+9% annuities, 2% occupational hazards (OH), the SAR 45,000 cap, payment by the 15th with a 2% monthly late fine, and pension age 60. The SANED conflict is resolved: GOSI's 2025 SANED booklet prints an amended Art. 7 that sets no fixed rate, and both SANED guides give 0.75% + 0.75%. Form 7 also says commissions count in the GOSI wage and in-kind housing is valued at two months' basic, so the spec differs on both. It gives a non-Saudi wage floor of SAR 400, which the spec does not have.

I read only and edited nothing. I used Read and Grep only, with no web access, and worked from the `.txt` file next to each PDF (`pdftotext -layout`). Arabic right-to-left extraction mixes up line order in places. The one sentence I had to rebuild is marked (Form 7, in-kind housing), and someone should check it against the PDF page.
