It also reads the official Arabic texts of the Law, Royal Decree M/273 and Council of Ministers (CoM) Resolution 1022, all from the Umm al-Qura gazette. It compares them with `docs/specs/payroll.md`, `docs/specs/hr.md` and the two earlier reports (`gosi-check-2026-10-17.md`, `gosi-check-legacy-2026-10-17.md`).

**The GCC insurance extension law was not found** (see Sources). Every GCC tag stays.

I edited no spec. This report lists the changes; the spec-writer applies them, and a human verifier still moves `StatutoryParameter` rows to `verified` (hr R7). Quotes are my English renderings of the Arabic; **the Arabic text governs**.

### What the payroll build must apply (summary of the differences)
1. **Joining and leaving month: contributions on days of service** (Regs Art. 20(2)). The spec prorates pay (R10) but says nothing about the GOSI wage. Add a rule.
2. **Contributions continue during leave and authorised unpaid absence, on the full registered wage, before any deduction** (Regs Art. 20(1), 20(3)(b), (c)). Unpaid-leave deductions (R16), sick-leave deductions (R17), loans and penalties must **not** reduce the GOSI wage. This also gives the official source for "contributions continue during maternity" (P20(e); OQ-44(b) closed).
3. **The contributory wage is a closed list** (Regs Art. 17):
   - basic, with commission, a percentage of sales and **a percentage of profits** counted as basic;
   - cash housing at the agreed value;
   - in-kind housing at "the basic wage for two months".

   This is now a primary source for F7's seeds. **Differs:** a bonus set as a percentage of profits counts, so a blanket `BONUS` False is wrong for that case. Leave encashment, notice pay and EOSB are outside the list, so the OQ-24 default is confirmed.
4. **Commission or piece-rate pay uses last year's monthly average, not the current month's actual** (Regs Art. 18(2)). Daily pay is × 30 (Art. 18(1)). New rule.
5. **Multiple employers: the SAR 45,000 cap applies across all employers, reduced pro rata** (Regs Art. 19). This matters for group staff on two entities' payrolls. New rule.
6. **OH stops while a contributor is outside the Kingdom for non-work reasons**, from the 1st of the month after departure until the month of return (Regs Art. 23). Crews and work travel are excepted. New rule; it affects expatriate long leave abroad.
7. **Payment by the 15th of the following month** (Regs Art. 24(1)): `gosi.payment_due_day` (`new_m273`) is now verifiable.
8. **Registration and data-change deadlines are now primary** (Regs Art. 6):
   - employee joining, leaving and data changes: by the last day of that month;
   - establishment registration and establishment data changes: 10 days.

   **No wage correction after that deadline** except by the Governor (Regs Art. 21). R57's alert must say "by the last day of this month".
9. **Secondment (إعارة) to another covered establishment: the receiving establishment applies the Law** (Regs Art. 20(3)(a)). Each group entity with its own legal personality is a separate establishment (Regs Art. 11(1)). This **differs** from R35, where the employing entity keeps paying GOSI for intra-KSA secondments. **Needs expert sign-off: legal.**
10. **Late fine 2% confirmed; waivers now known** (Law Art. 9(3); Regs Art. 25): full waiver for a delay of 10 days or less (Governor), up to 50% (Governor), more (Board). R37 stays manual.
11. **CoM 1022 Clause Third(5) and (6) are in force by the CoM resolution itself.** These are the 180-month minimum for a pension and SANED at 1.5% (0.75% + 0.75%). CoM 1022 says the draft Royal Decree covers only paragraphs (1) to (4) and (7) of Clause Third; (5) and (6) rest on the CoM's own powers under Law Art. 16(2) and 44(2). SANED can be reset every three years by ±0.5% within 0.5% to 2% (Regs Art. 74).
12. **Annuities steps 18% to 22% confirmed** in the official Arabic RD Clause Third(1), (2). **The step month (July or August) is still not settled**: the Regs are silent.
13. **GCC nationals: still no text.** Keep refusing `gcc` and the GCC-state Saudi `local_entity_employee` case.
